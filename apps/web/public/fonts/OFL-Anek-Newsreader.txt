Subsetting, and why the files are small
---------------------------------------
Anek Devanagari ships at 709KB and is carried here at 42KB, because this
site sets exactly two Devanagari words. Anek Latin and Newsreader are cut to
the Latin characters the site actually renders, plus U+20B9. The variable
axes are intact in every file and that is the point of using them: Anek
keeps wght 100-800 and wdth 75-125, Newsreader keeps wght 200-800 and
opsz 6-72. A static instance would have been smaller and would have cost the
design its width and optical-size axes, which are the reason these families
were chosen.
